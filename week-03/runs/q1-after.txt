Overdraft and returned item fees
When Larkfield pays a check, debit card purchase, or automatic payment that overdraws a member's checking account, the overdraft fee is $29 per item paid. When Larkfield returns an item unpaid because the account does not have enough money, the returned item fee is $29 per item returned. No more than three overdraft or returned item fees are charged to one account in a single business day. No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day. Members can link a savings account for overdraft transfers. An overdraft transfer from a linked savings account is free.

Checking account monthly fees
Everyday Checking has no monthly service fee. Plus Checking has a monthly service fee of $8. The Plus Checking fee is waived for any month in which the account keeps a minimum daily balance of $1,000, or receives direct deposits totaling $500 or more.

How much the member may have to pay
A member's share of the loss from unauthorized debit card transactions depends on how quickly they report it. If the member reports a lost or stolen card within two business days of learning about it, they pay no more than $50. If they report it later than that, they may pay up to $500. If unauthorized transactions appear on a statement and the member does not report them within 60 days after the statement was sent, they may have to pay for transactions after those 60 days in full.

Dormant accounts
An account with no member-initiated activity for 24 months becomes dormant. A dormant account with a balance under $500 is charged $5 per month. Any deposit or withdrawal by the member ends dormant status.
