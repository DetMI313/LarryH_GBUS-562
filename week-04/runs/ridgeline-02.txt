Claude: I'm sorry to hear about your grandmother's passing. Please accept my condolences.

Regarding time off: according to our Bereavement and Family Leave policy, for the death of a grandparent you're entitled to up to 3 paid days off (bereavement-and-family-leave.html). These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but must be used within 60 days, and this leave does not reduce your PTO or sick balance.

To request the time, just let your supervisor know as soon as you can — no documentation is required (bereavement-and-family-leave.html).

If you need additional time beyond the 3 days, you're welcome to use PTO or discuss options with your supervisor or People and Culture.

Wishing you and your family comfort during this difficult time.
