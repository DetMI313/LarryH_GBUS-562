## Reply
Hi Dana,

You currently have **9 vacation days** left.

On carrying days over: you can carry up to **5 unused vacation days** into the next calendar year. Those days must be used within the first three months of that year or they are lost. Any unused days above the 5-day limit are lost at the end of the year and are not paid out. *(leave-policy.html, "Carrying vacation into next year")*

So if you don't use any more days this year, 5 would carry over and 4 would be lost. You'd need to take at least 4 days before year-end to keep all of them.
